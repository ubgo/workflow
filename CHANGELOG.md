# Changelog

All notable changes to `github.com/ubgo/workflow` and its modules (`admin`, `cli`, `hatchet`, `otel`, `redis`, `sentry`, `sqlstore`) are documented here. The format follows Keep a Changelog, and versioning will be SemVer with the pre-GA convention that `v0.x` may break. Nothing is tagged yet; everything below is on `main`.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- The core contract: `Client`, `Workflow`, `Step` (run, wait for event, sleep), `Job`, `Schedule`, retry and concurrency policies, capabilities (`Caps`), errors, `Validate`, `Define` for typed JSON handlers and `StepInfo` on the step context. Standard library only, guarded by `deps_test.go`.
- `clock`: the injected time source, `Real` and `Fake` (timers fire on `Advance`).
- `cron`: an own five-field cron parser and next-run maths in an explicit time zone, with written DST rules.
- `memory`: the in-process driver, with every capability except durability.
- `middleware`: step middleware (logging, recovery, timeout, codec, error throttling), enqueue middleware (unique, unique until the run finishes, debounce, route), the `WithCodec` client wrapper, and claim check for big payloads with a pluggable `BlobStore`.
- The run-finished hook (`RunNotifier`, `RunEvent`, `CapRunFinished`).
- Plugins (`Plugin`, `Use`): step middleware, enqueue middleware and run-finished observers bundled behind one name.
- `batch`: batches of jobs with Then, Catch and Finally callbacks and progress on any `Client`, pluggable completion sources, callback retry, native groups through `GroupRunner` and `GroupSpawner`, and resumption of a dispatch whose process died (`Manager.ResumeDispatches`).
- `failover`: a `Client` over a primary and a secondary backend, with an injectable classifier, required idempotency keys, prefixed run references and a cool-off.
- `workflowtest`: the conformance suite every driver runs, `Fake` with fluent assertions, and `Recorder`, which wraps a real client and records its enqueues.
- `hatchet` module: the Hatchet driver on the `sdks/go` SDK, with rate limits, bulk-trigger batch groups, a label-based batch completion source, `RunFinder`, and an opt-in batch coordinator (`Config.BatchCoordinator`).
- `otel` module: OpenTelemetry step and enqueue middleware, with trace context carried from Enqueue to step, step and enqueue metrics, and opt-in label attributes.
- `sqlstore` module: the durable SQL driver for SQLite, Postgres and MySQL 8 through `ubgo/sqlb`, with every capability; versioned migrations on sqlb's `contrib/migrate` ledger; expiring leases and abandonment of poison steps; a durable `batch.Store`; opt-in retention and archiving of finished runs; transactional enqueue (`EnqueueTx`); and a durable `UniqueStore`, usable without the SQL driver.
- `sqlstore/outbox`: a transactional outbox and relay for any `Client`, at least once.
- Retry jitter (`Backoff.Jitter`, `CapRetryJitter`) and per-error retry delays (`RetryPolicy.DelayFor`, `RetryAfter`, `RetryAfterDelay`, `CapRetryDelay`), resolved by one `RetryPolicy.NextDelay`.
- Cron misfire policies (`Misfire` with `MisfireSkip`, `MisfireFireOnce`, `MisfireFireAll`, `Within`, `Max`; `Misfire.Due`; `MisfireGrace`), one capability each (`CapMisfireSkip`, `CapMisfireFireOnce`, `CapMisfireFireAll`).
- `Workflow.CronLocation` for declared crons in a time zone, with `CapCronZone`; `RequiresSchedule` and `CheckScheduleCaps` check a schedule's needs against a driver.
- `ErrUnavailable`, `Unavailable` and `IsUnavailable`, a shared "backend unavailable" error for outages.
- The reserved label prefixes in one list in core: `ReservedLabelPrefixes`, `ReservedLabel`, `ValidateLabels`, `LabelPrefixInternal`, `LabelPrefixBatch`, `LabelPrefixOTel`.
- `cron.Parser`, and `memory.WithCronParser` and `memory.WithJitterSource`; `workflowtest.FakeCronParser`, and conformance groups `retryseam` and `misfire`.
- `middleware`: `DebounceStore`, `MemoryDebounce` and `WithDebounceStore`; `CircuitStore`, `MemoryCircuit` and `ThrottleConfig.Store`; `WithLogLevel` for `Logging`; `Encrypt(key, oldKeys...)` decrypts with retired keys for rotation.
- `batch`: `Batch.Labels`, saved in State and copied onto Then, Catch and Finally jobs; `WithIDFunc` for batch IDs.
- `failover`: `StrictClassifier`, which fails over only on errors marked `workflow.Unavailable`; `WithObserver` for failovers, primary skips and cool-off start and end.
- `otel`: `WithParenting` (`ParentChild`, `ParentLink`, `ParentNewRootLink`), `WithStepSpanName`, `WithEnqueueSpanName`, and `WithMetricLabels` for opt-in label dimensions on metrics.
- `hatchet`: `Config.Logger`, `Config.Clock` and the tunings `GroupChunkRuns`, `GroupChunkBytes`, `BatchCoordinatorMaxInput`, `BatchCoordinatorRetry`, `RegisterTimeout` and `VisibilityLag`, each defaulting to the old fixed value; errors from an unreachable engine match `workflow.ErrUnavailable`.
- `sqlstore`: `WithErrorClassifier`, `DefaultErrorClassifier` and `ErrorClass` (connection failures match `workflow.ErrUnavailable`); `WithIDGenerator` and `IDKind`; `WithSchema` (Postgres only); `MigrationScripts`, with `MigrationOwner` and `UniqueMigrationOwner` exported; `WithTxAttempts`, `WithTickRetention`, `WithHousekeepingEvery` and `WithPruneChunk`; `Retention.KeepLast`; `WithCronParser` and `WithJitterSource`; the capabilities `CapRetryJitter`, `CapRetryDelay`, `CapCronZone` and the three misfire capabilities.
- `sqlstore.DebounceStore` (`NewDebounceStore`, `Client.DebounceStore`), a durable `middleware.DebounceStore` so `Debounce` works across processes.
- `sqlstore/outbox`: `WithOnDead`, `ListDead` and `DeadQuery`, `Requeue`, `AllowDuplicates`, `WithSchema`, `WithIDGenerator` and `MigrationScripts`.
- Named queues: `Workflow.Queue`, `Job.Queue`, `QueueConfig` (workers and priority per queue), `DefaultQueue`, `EffectiveQueue`, `ValidateQueues`; `CapQueue` and `CapQueueOverride`; `memory.WithQueues`.
- Per-job and bounded retries: `Job.Retry` (`CapJobRetry`), `RetryPolicy.Until` (`CapRetryUntil`) and `RetryPolicy.MaxErrors` (`CapRetryMaxErrors`), with `Attempts`, `RetryPolicy.Next` and `EffectiveRetriesFor`; `workflow.Discard`, `IsDiscarded`, `DiscardedError` and `ErrDiscarded` end a run cancelled rather than failed (`CapDiscard`).
- Run management interfaces: `RunQuerier` (`Runs` with `RunQuery`, `RunInfo`, `RunPage`; `RunQuery.Ref` fetches one run by the ref `Enqueue` returned), `RunRetrier`, `RunDeleter` and `BulkCanceller`, with `ErrWrongState`; `Pauser` with `PauseTarget` (`PauseWorkflow`, `PauseQueue`, `PauseAll`) and `CheckPause`; `Drainer` (`Drain`, `Done`) and `memory.WithStopWhenIdle`. One capability each (`CapRunQuery`, `CapRetryRun`, `CapDeleteRun`, `CapCancelAll`, `CapPauseWorkflow`, `CapPauseQueue`, `CapDrain`).
- Schedule control: `Workflow.ScheduleWhen`, `Schedule.Windows` and `Workflow.CronWindows` (`Window`, `Windows.Allows`); `TickNotifier` with `Tick` (fired, skipped with a reason, misfired with a count) and `FiredLabels` (`LabelSchedule`, `LabelScheduleName` on every fired run); `ScheduleLister` (`ScheduleInfo` with the next fire); `SchedulePauser` (`ScheduleTarget`); `AttachScheduleHooks` with `ScheduleHooks` (`Fired`, `Skipped`, `OnSuccess`, `OnFailure`, `After`). One capability each (`CapScheduleWhen`, `CapScheduleWindow`, `CapTickEvents`, `CapScheduleList`, `CapSchedulePause`).
- `workflow.RunInline` runs a workflow in the calling goroutine with no driver, with `InlineMiddleware`, `InlineSleep`, `InlineNow` and `InlineEvents`; `workflow.Forward`, an embeddable forwarder of every optional client interface for wrappers; `StepInfo.Queue` and `StepInfo.ReadyAt`.
- `memory.WithRunRetention(RunRetention{MaxAge, MaxRuns})`, `DefaultRunRetention`.
- `cron.Builder`: named frequencies (`cron.DailyAt("9:00").Weekdays()`, `EveryMinutes(15)`, `WeeklyOn`, `Quarterly`, ...) that write a validated five-field expression, with `cron.ErrBuilder`.
- `middleware.Release(when, delay)` puts a step back for a delay without running it, as a `workflow.RetryAfter` wrapping `middleware.ErrReleased`; `middleware.UniqueUntilStarted` frees a unique job's lock when a step of its run starts, with `ReleaseUniqueOnStart` as its step half for a separate worker process; `middleware.Pinger` (`NewPinger`, `HTTPPing`, `PingFunc`, `WithPingTimeout`, `WithPingLogger`) sends heartbeat pings from schedule hooks.
- `batch`: `Manager.Add` adds jobs to a running batch; `Manager.RetryFailed` (with `WithRetryableJobs`) re-runs a finished batch's failed jobs and re-arms its callbacks; `Before` and `Progress` callbacks; `Manager.Prune` with `PruneFilter` on an optional `Pruner` store; `Manager.AwaitStep`, a step that dispatches a batch and waits for it (`ErrBatchPending`, `ErrBatchFailed`); `EnqueueMany` for bulk enqueue without a batch (`ManyEnqueuer`, then `GroupRunner`, then a loop); `JobLabels` and `LabelAttempt`; `ErrClosed` and `ErrDispatching`.
- `workflowtest`: batch assertions (`AssertBatched`, `AssertBatchCount`, `AssertNothingBatched`) and `AssertChained` on `Fake` and `Recorder`; `FakeExcept` and `FakeOnly` pass chosen workflows through to a real client; `RunStep` tests one step's outcome (released, failed, permanent, discarded). The Fake records `Retry`, `Delete` and `Pause` (`AssertRetried`, `AssertDeleted`, `AssertPaused`, `AssertNotPaused`), checks job queues and per-job retry like a driver (`AssertEnqueuedOn`, `HasQueue`, `HasRetry`), and claims the memory driver's new capabilities except those it does not serve; the Recorder records `Retry` and `Delete` with the same assertions. Seven new conformance groups cover queues, retry limits, discard, run management, pauses, schedule control and drain.
- `failover.Client` implements the run query, retry, delete, bulk cancel, pause, schedule list and pause, tick and drain interfaces: run calls route by the ref prefix, `Runs` merges both backends into one paged feed, and the schedule family follows where schedules live.
- `admin` module (`github.com/ubgo/workflow/admin`, standard library and the core only): Go operations over any driver (`New`, `Runs`, `Run`, `Failures`, `Retry`, `Delete`, `Cancel`, `CancelAll`, `Pause`, `Resume`, `Paused`, `Schedules`, `QueueStats`, `QueueDepths`, each refusing what the driver lacks), a closed-by-default JSON API (`NewHandler` with typed `Auth` hooks: `Identify` before routing, `Authorize` per action and resource, JSON-only writes, payloads hidden unless `WithPayloads`), and a queue threshold `Monitor` that alerts once on crossing and once on resolve.
- `otel`: the `workflow.queue` attribute (`AttrQueue`), the `workflow.queue.wait` histogram (`MetricQueueWait`, `QueueWaitBuckets`) and the `workflow.queue.depth` gauge (`MetricQueueDepth`, `Instrumentation.ObserveQueueDepth`).
- `hatchet`: named queues (`Workflow.Queue`, `Config.Queues`: one Hatchet worker per queue, `Workers` as slots), `Runs` (filtered run list with failed step, attempts and payload; unfired delayed starts included), `CancelAll`, `Retry` (Hatchet's replay of the failed steps; succeeded steps keep their outputs), `Schedules` (cron trigger listing with the next fire) and `Drain`/`Done`; `Config.API` and `NewAPI` for workflow and schedule pause, declared only when set.
- `sqlstore`: named queues (`WithQueues`, per-queue `Workers` and `Priority`), `Job.Retry` with `Until` and `MaxErrors`, `Discard`, `Runs`/`Retry`/`Delete`/`CancelAll`, durable `Pause`/`Resume` and `PauseSchedule`/`ResumeSchedule` shared by every client on the database, `Schedules`, `OnTick`, `ScheduleWhen` and schedule windows, `Drain` and `WithStopWhenIdle`, `EnqueueMany` in one transaction and `BatchStore.Prune`; migration `003_queues_admin` adds `run_ext`, `step_ext`, `pauses` and `schedule_ext` and backfills existing runs.

- Run states: `StatusRetrying` (a step waits on its retry backoff; `RunInfo.NextAttempt` and `NextAttemptAt` say which attempt comes and when, and `RunInfo.Error` holds the failed attempt's error) and `StatusDiscarded` (terminal, after `Discard`), behind `CapRunStates`, declared by memory and sqlstore; `EndStatus`, the one mapping from a step error to a run's end status; `Status.Valid`. `RunQuery.Statuses` accepts both; `batch.Batch.DiscardedAs` says how a batch counts a discarded job (failed by default) and `Progress.Discarded` counts them.
- Deferred dispatch in the core, standard library only: `Deferred` (`NewDeferred`, `Add`, `Flush`, `Discard`, `Len`), `WithDeferred`, `DeferredFrom`, the `DeferEnqueue` enqueue middleware, and `DeferHTTP` with `DeferOptions` and `DeferSucceeded`, which sends a request's jobs only when it answers below 400 and drops them on an error status or a panic; `ErrDeferredClosed`.
- Chains of jobs: `Job.Chain` runs each job after the one before it succeeds, as data stored with the run; a running step edits the rest with `PrependToChain` and `AppendToChain`, applied only when its attempt succeeds (`ChainEditor`, `WithChainEditor`, `ChainEditorFrom`, `ChainEdits` for drivers, `NextInChain`, `CloneJob`, `MaxChainJobs`), behind `CapChain`, declared by memory and sqlstore. Codecs and the claim check rewrite chained payloads, including jobs added at run time.
- Worker balancing: `WorkerScaler` (`QueueLoads`, `SetWorkers`) behind `CapWorkerScale`, implemented by memory and sqlstore; `Balancer`, `BalancerFunc`, the `ByDepth`, `ByWait` and `Proportional` strategies with `BalanceConfig` and `QueueBounds`; `BalanceLoop` (`Once`, `Run`, injected clock, `DefaultBalanceEvery`). Off unless an app runs a loop.
- Closures on the in-process driver: `ClosureJob`, `EnqueueClosure`, `ClosureEnqueuer`, `ValidateClosure`, `ClosureWorkflow`, `ClosureStep` and `LabelClosure`, behind `CapClosure`, declared by memory only; the SQL and Hatchet drivers refuse with an `UnsupportedError`.
- `workflow.Forward` forwards `WorkerScaler` and `ClosureEnqueuer`; `failover` declares `CapRunStates` and `CapChain` when both backends do, and sends closures and worker scaling to the primary.
- `workflowtest`: conformance groups `runstates`, `chain`, `scale` and `closure`; the `Fake` records closures (`EnqueueClosure`, `Closures`, `AssertClosureEnqueued`, `RunClosures`), matches chains with `HasChain` and ends a run with `Finish(ref, StatusDiscarded)`; `RunStep` takes `StepChain` and `StepCaps` and reports the edited chain in `StepOutcome.Chain` and `ChainNames`.
- `batch.Dispatch` and `Manager.Add` refuse a job that carries a `Job.Chain` (a batch tracks one run per job), and `Validate` refuses the reserved workflow name `ubgo.workflow.closure`.
- `sqlstore`: the run states, chains and worker scaling. A run in backoff reads `StatusRetrying` on every client; a discarded run is stored `discarded` and keeps its reason; `Retention.Cancelled` also governs discarded runs. `Job.Chain` and a step's edits are written in the transaction that records the step's success, and the next job is enqueued in the transaction that marks the run succeeded; when the next job's workflow is not registered on the finishing client, the chain waits until a client that registers it hands it on; `Retry` of a failed run resumes its chain. `QueueLoads` and `SetWorkers` cover each `WithQueues` queue with this client's counts (not stored); an uncapped queue reports the pool size. `CapClosure` is not declared: a Go function cannot be stored.
- `hatchet`: declares none of `CapRunStates`, `CapChain`, `CapWorkerScale` and `CapClosure`. A run in backoff reads queued and a discarded run failed, as before; a `Runs` or `CancelAll` filter naming `StatusRetrying` or `StatusDiscarded` is refused (field `RunQuery.Statuses`) rather than answered with an empty page; `Job.Chain` is refused at `Enqueue`, `RunGroup` and `SpawnGroup`, and `PrependToChain`/`AppendToChain` in a Hatchet step return `UnsupportedError`.
- `admin`: `Admin.Discarded` (operation `discarded`, needs `CapRunQuery` and `CapRunStates`) lists discarded runs beside `Failures`, and the JSON API serves it at `GET /discarded` (action `discarded.list`); `QueueStats` adds each queue's `Workers` and `Running` on a client with `CapWorkerScale` (`QueueReport.Workers`), and `GET /queues` rows carry `workers` and `running`; run JSON carries `next_attempt` and `next_attempt_at` while a run is retrying.
- `otel`: the step outcome `discarded` (`OutcomeDiscarded`) on `workflow.step.outcomes`, `workflow.step.duration` and the step span; `Instrumentation.RunFinished()` is a run-finished observer counting run ends on `workflow.runs.finished` (`MetricRunsFinished`) by `workflow.name` and `workflow.run.status` (`AttrRunStatus`).

- Durable closures by name: `workflow.NewFunc` builds a `Func[In]` (`Name`, `Workflow`, `Job`, `Enqueue`), a Go function registered under a name at start-up (`RegisterFunc`, `FuncDefinition`) and enqueued by that name with a JSON argument, also by name alone (`FuncJob`); options `FuncQueue`, `FuncRetry`, `FuncTimeout` and `FuncCatch` (runs once with the decoded argument when a run fails for good). A `Func` is a one-step workflow (step `FuncStep`), so it runs on memory, sqlstore and Hatchet with no driver change and survives a restart on a durable driver; `EnqueueClosure` stays the in-process quick form.
- Task output: `middleware.CaptureOutput` captures the log records a step writes through `middleware.OutputLogger` and its outcome, and delivers an `Output` after each attempt to an `OutputSink`: `WriterSink`, `FileSink` with `FileReplace` or `FileAppend` (created 0600), or an `OutputSinkFunc` such as the app's own mailer; `FormatOutput` is their text format. Options `CaptureResult` (the step's result, off by default), `CaptureOnlyFailures`, `CaptureLevel`, `CaptureTee`, `CaptureOnly`, `CaptureOnSinkError` and `CaptureClock`. A sink error never fails the step.
- Worker recycling: `workflow.Recycle` (`MaxJobs`, `MaxTime`, `MaxMemory`, `Memory`, `CheckEvery`), `ValidateRecycle`, `HeapObjectBytes`, `DefaultRecycleCheck`, `ErrRecycle`, `RecycleError` with `RecycleReason`, `RecycleWatch`, `StopCauser` and `Serve`: at a limit the client drains and stops itself, and `Serve` returns the `*RecycleError` so a supervisor restarts the process. `memory.WithRecycle`, `sqlstore.WithRecycle` (runs never claimed stay queued) and `hatchet.Config.Recycle` (workers paused on the engine; up to `Slots` attempts already handed out may start past `MaxJobs`; proven live by `TestRecycleLive`). `workflow.Forward`, `middleware.WrapClient`/`WithCodec` and `failover` pass `StopCause` through (failover joins both sides).
- Label prefix search: `RunQuery.LabelPrefixes` (a value prefix per key; an empty prefix means the key is present), capability `CapLabelPrefix` and `CheckRunQuery`, which refuses the filter on a driver without the capability. memory and sqlstore declare it; failover only when both backends do; Hatchet refuses it in `Runs` and `CancelAll` (the engine matches metadata by exact value only).
- `sqlstore`: a label index. Migration `005_run_labels` adds `<prefix>run_labels`, one row per label of a run (key and value cut to 191 characters), indexed on key, value and run; enqueue writes the rows in the run's transaction, and delete and retention remove them. `RunQuery.Labels` and `LabelPrefixes` in `Runs` and `CancelAll` read the index (a prefix as `LIKE` with its wildcards escaped), and exact values are still checked in Go. `Client.IndexLabels` backfills runs written before 005 or by an older client, in chunks under a watermark in the locks row `labels:indexed`, safe to run twice and from several processes; `Migrate` calls it after the scripts.
- `admin`: a web UI served by the admin handler at `ui/` under its mount point, behind the same `Auth` (new read-only action `ActionUI`, `ui.read`): queues with depth, wait and workers; runs filtered by status, workflow, queue and label, with numbered pages and a page size; run detail; failures and discarded runs with retry and delete; schedules; pause and resume of queues, workflows and schedules; keyboard keys, light and dark. Three embedded files with no third-party code and nothing fetched at run time; pages carry a strict Content-Security-Policy, `X-Frame-Options: DENY` and `nosniff`; writes stay JSON-only.
- `admin`: `POST /schedules/{id}/pause` and `/resume` (`ActionPauseSchedule`, `ActionResumeSchedule`), `OpSchedulePause`, `Admin.PauseSchedule` and `Admin.ResumeSchedule`; `count=1` on the run list routes returns `total`, `truncated` and each page's `cursors`, from `Admin.CountRuns` (`RunCount`, bounded by `WithMaxScan`); label prefix search with `label=key=prefix*` on the run list routes and `label_prefixes` in the bulk-cancel body, refused with 501 on a driver without `CapLabelPrefix`.
- `workflowtest`: conformance groups `func` (a `Func` by name with retry, `FuncJob`, `FuncCatch` once with its argument, an unregistered name refused; every driver), `retryingerror` (while a run is retrying, `RunInfo.Error` holds the failed attempt's error and `FailedStep` is empty) and `labelprefix`, and `done` (after `Done` closes, `Enqueue`, `Signal` and `Schedule` return `ErrNotStarted`).
- Cron overlap policy: `Schedule.Overlap` and `Workflow.CronOverlap` take `OverlapSkip` to skip a tick while the schedule's previous run is unfinished, decided from the run's stored status (`OverlapPolicy.Blocks`), not a node-local lock, and reported as `TickSkipped` with `TickReasonOverlap`; capability `CapOverlapSkip`. The default stays `OverlapAllow`.
- Trigger a schedule now: `ScheduleTriggerer.TriggerSchedule(ctx, id)` runs a schedule with its input and labels (plus `LabelTrigger=manual`, `TriggeredLabels`), obeys `OverlapSkip` (`ErrOverlap`, `OverlapRefusal`), ignores windows, `ScheduleWhen` and a pause, and leaves the next cron fire alone; `Tick.Manual`; capability `CapScheduleTrigger`.
- Last-fire bookkeeping: `ScheduleInfo.Last` (`ScheduleLast{Fire, Run, Status}`) and `ScheduleInfo.Overlap`; capability `CapScheduleHistory`.
- Enqueue-only clients: capability `CapEnqueueOnly` for a driver mode that enqueues and reads status without `Start` or workers, so web nodes enqueue and worker nodes run; `workflowtest.Instance.EnqueueOnly`. The memory driver has none, since its runs live in the process.
- Error history per attempt: `RunInfo.Errors` (`AttemptError{Step, Attempt, Error, At, Stack}`), bounded per run (`DefaultErrorHistory` 20, `AppendAttemptError`, `NewAttemptError`), stacks from errors implementing `StackTracer` (`StackOf`, cut at `MaxErrorStack`); `WithErrorHistory(n)` on memory and sqlstore; capability `CapErrorHistory`.
- Shutdown requeue: capability `CapShutdownRequeue` for durable drivers that put a step cut short by a clean stop back without spending an attempt (same `StepInfo.Attempt`, not counted in `RunInfo.Attempts`, the retry limit or the error history), proven by a conformance group.
- Force a run to an end: `RunForcer.ForceComplete(ctx, ref)` (an unfinished or failed run becomes succeeded; its key is freed and its chain continues) and `ForceFail(ctx, ref, reason)` (no retry, no `OnFailure`; `ErrForced`, `ForcedFailure`, `CanForceComplete`, `CanForceFail`); capability `CapForceRun`.
- Failure reporting: `workflow.Failure`, `workflow.Reporter`/`ReporterFunc` and `FailureOf`; `RunEvent.Step` names the failed step; the `middleware.Report` plugin (`ReportFailures`, `ReportAttempts`, `ReportConfig{Attempts, Discarded, Clock}`). The payload is never reported.
- Failure alerts: `middleware.Alerts` and `AlertPlugin` call an app-supplied `Alerter` (`AlerterFunc`) on final failures, deduplicated and rate limited through a pluggable `AlertLimiter` (`MemoryAlertLimiter`, `AlertLimits{Dedup, Max, Per}`; by default one alert per workflow and step every `DefaultAlertDedup`, 10 minutes); a limiter error still sends the alert.
- Unique by arguments: `middleware.UniqueByArgs(opts...)`, a key function for `Unique` from the workflow and the SHA-256 of the canonical JSON payload, with `ArgsFields`, `ArgsQueue`, `ArgsLabels` and `ArgsKeyPrefix`.
- `workflow.Forward` forwards `ScheduleTriggerer` and `RunForcer`; `failover` implements both (trigger on the primary, force routed by the ref's prefix); `workflowtest.Fake` records `TriggerSchedule`, `ForceComplete` and `ForceFail` (`AssertTriggered`, `AssertForced`, `Triggers`, `Forced`, `FakeSchedule.LastRun`).
- `workflowtest`: conformance groups `overlap`, `trigger`, `schedhistory`, `enqueueonly`, `errorhistory`, `shutdownrequeue` and `force`.
- `sentry` module (`github.com/ubgo/workflow/sentry`, go 1.25): a `workflow.Reporter` on sentry-go, one event per failure with the tags `workflow.name`, `workflow.step`, `workflow.status` and `workflow.final` and a `workflow` context, never the payload, and labels only on opt-in (`WithLabels`, `WithAllLabels`); the default fingerprint groups by workflow, step and error type; `WithLevels`, `WithFingerprint`, `WithEvent`.
- `redis` module (`github.com/ubgo/workflow/redis`, go 1.24): a durable Redis driver on go-redis v9. Runs, schedules, leases and idempotency keys are Redis keys under a hash-tagged prefix (`WithPrefix`), and every state change is one Lua script. It declares `CapDurable`, `CapDelayedStart`, `CapDurableSleep`, `CapIdempotencyKey`, `CapCron`, `CapCancel`, `CapRetryJitter`, `CapRetryDelay`, `CapJobRetry`, `CapRetryUntil`, `CapRetryMaxErrors`, the three misfire policies, `CapCronZone`, `CapRunQuery`, `CapDeleteRun`, `CapDrain`, `CapScheduleList`, `CapScheduleTrigger`, `CapScheduleHistory`, `CapOverlapSkip`, `CapEnqueueOnly`, `CapErrorHistory`, `CapShutdownRequeue` and `CapForceRun`, each proven by the conformance suite, and refuses wait steps, concurrency keys, rate limits, queues, chains, pauses, run-finished events and the rest with an `UnsupportedError`.
- `redis`: a run is claimed whole under a renewed lease; writes are fenced by the claim token, so a worker that lost its lease writes nothing; a dead worker's runs are claimed again when the lease expires; `WithMaxReclaims` (default 5) fails a run that keeps killing its workers with `ErrAbandoned`; a clean stop hands runs back without spending an attempt. Cron ticks fire once across any number of clients (a compare-and-set on the schedule's last catch-up time creates the runs in the same script), with the overlap policy and `TriggerSchedule` decided in that script; a named schedule upserts by (Workflow, Name); fixed-offset zones are stored by offset. Options `WithPrefix`, `WithClock`, `WithLeaseClock`, `WithLease`, `WithPollInterval`, `WithWorkers`, `WithMaxReclaims`, `WithRetention` (finished-run TTL, 24 hours), `WithLogger`, `WithMiddleware`, `WithCronParser`, `WithJitterSource`, `WithIDFunc`, `WithEnqueueOnly`, `WithErrorHistory`.
- `bench` module (`github.com/ubgo/workflow/bench`, not a library) and `docs/reference/benchmarks.md`: enqueue throughput, end-to-end throughput and pickup latency for memory, sqlstore (SQLite, Postgres, Postgres with LISTEN/NOTIFY), Redis and Hatchet, run one system at a time by `task bench`. Measured on one laptop with 10 workers and a separate producer: sqlstore on Postgres picks up a new job in 7 ms median with LISTEN/NOTIFY against 415 ms when it only polls, and runs about 1,100 to 1,200 jobs/s end to end at 10 to 100 workers (96 at 100 workers before the throughput fix); the Redis driver in under 1 ms, about 5,500 jobs/s end to end; Hatchet in about 13 ms. The benchmark also found the sqlstore pool deadlock, the throughput collapse with many workers on Postgres and the 30-second pickup stall, all fixed.
- `sqlstore`: `WithNotifier(Notifier)` wakes idle clients on other machines when work becomes due (an enqueue, a finished step, a retry, a resume); the package `sqlstore/pgnotify` implements it on Postgres LISTEN/NOTIFY (`pgnotify.New(db)`; `Listen` needs the pgx stdlib driver, else `pgnotify.ErrDriver`; it holds one pool connection and does not work behind a transaction-mode pooler). Polling stays as the fallback and the default is unchanged. ### Changed

- `hatchet`: the error text the engine stores for a failure the driver ends without retry (`Permanent`, `Discard`, `NoRetryOn`, a wait timeout) now starts with `[ubgo.workflow: not retried] `; `RunInfo.Error`, `RunEvent.Err` and admin show the step's own text, Hatchet's dashboard shows the prefix. Runs failed before this change have no marker: a `Permanent` failure of such a run with retries left reads queued for `FailedSettle` after it failed, then failed.
- `ValidateSchedule` refuses reserved-prefix labels and malformed misfire policies; `Validate` refuses a `Backoff.Jitter` outside [0, 1], `CronLocation = time.Local`, and a `Misfire` without a `Cron`. Defaults are unchanged.

- `middleware.WrapClient` and `WithCodec` forward every optional client interface through `workflow.Forward`.
- `otel`: step metrics and spans gain the `workflow.queue` attribute; the enqueue span and counter gain it when `Job.Queue` is set.
- `middleware.Recover` returns an error that implements `workflow.StackTracer` (its message is unchanged and it still matches `ErrPanic`).
- `memory`: a run that a `Cancel` or a force already ended no longer runs `OnFailure` when an attempt still in flight fails afterwards.
- `hatchet`: a client config that does not load (a missing token) makes `Start` return an error instead of panicking inside the SDK.
- `sqlstore`: a run ended by `ForceFail` is recorded with an explicit flag (`<prefix>run_forced`, in migration `006_schedule_history`), and its `RunEvent.Err` matches `workflow.ErrForced` only through that flag, never from the error text; `Retry` and `ForceComplete` clear it.
- `sqlstore`: on Postgres and MySQL an enqueue admits its own run inside its insert transaction (workflows without a concurrency or rate policy) instead of every enqueue sweeping all pending runs, which made concurrent enqueuers contend for the same rows; SQLite keeps the sweep, which measured faster on its single writer. Idle workers are woken one at a time, each successful claim waking the next, instead of all at once; with 100 idle workers this cut database transactions per job from 254 to 44. Measured with the bench harness on the host Postgres (5,000 jobs, 256 B, end-to-end jobs/s): 10 workers 637 to 1,441, 50 workers 244 to 1,471, 100 workers 156 to 1,382.
- `memory`: a failed run's error is a `*workflow.RunError` wrapping the old message; wait and step timeouts also match `ErrWaitTimeout`/`ErrStepTimeout` and `ErrTimeout` (they still match `context.DeadlineExceeded`).
- `redis`: a failed run's `RunEvent.Err` is a `*workflow.RunError` and `RunInfo.Error` its message; a step timeout matches `ErrStepTimeout` and `ErrTimeout` as well as `context.DeadlineExceeded`.

### Changed (breaking)

- Reserved labels are refused, not rewritten: `middleware.Unique`/`UniqueUntilFinished` and `ClaimCheck.Enqueue` refuse incoming `ubgo.workflow/` labels, `batch.Manager.Dispatch` refuses `workflow.batch.` labels on jobs and in `Batch.Labels`, and the `otel` `Enqueue` middleware refuses `otel.` labels (before, it dropped them silently), all with `workflow.ErrInvalid`. Use each of these middlewares once per chain.
- `sqlstore`: `Client.Schedule` with a `Name` upserts by (Workflow, Name) instead of adding a row per call.
- `sqlstore`: new migrations `002_schedule_misfire` and, in the list `ubgo/workflow/sqlstore/debounce`, `001_debounce`. Run `Migrate` before starting the new version.
- `hatchet`: capability refusals happen at `Register` (they were at `Start`).
- `sqlstore/outbox`: `NewRelay` refuses a target without `CapIdempotencyKey` unless `AllowDuplicates` is given.
- `failover`: `DefaultClassifier` fails over on an error marked `workflow.Unavailable` even when it wraps `context.DeadlineExceeded`; the caller's own cancelled context still never fails over.
- `sqlstore`: new migration `003_queues_admin`. Run `Migrate` before starting the new version.
- `sqlstore`: new migration `004_run_states_chain` (the `<prefix>run_chain` and `<prefix>step_err` tables, and runs discarded by earlier releases rewritten from cancelled to discarded). Run `Migrate` before starting the new version.
- `memory`: finished runs are forgotten after 24 hours, keeping at most 10,000 (`DefaultRunRetention`), so `Status` of an older run returns `ErrNotFound`. Pass `memory.WithRunRetention(memory.RunRetention{})` to keep every run as before.
- `workflowtest`: `FakeCaps` and `FakeLike` now also drop the run query, bulk cancel, schedule pause, schedule listing, tick and drain capabilities, which the Fake cannot serve by itself.
- A tick that falls due while its schedule is paused never fires, not even on resume and whatever the misfire policy; it is reported once as skipped with reason `paused`.
- Two new run statuses (`CapRunStates`): on memory and sqlstore a run ended by `Discard` now reads `StatusDiscarded` (it was `StatusCancelled`; its `Err` still matches `ErrDiscarded` and `ErrCancelled`), and a run whose step waits on its retry backoff reads `StatusRetrying` (it was `StatusQueued`). Code that switches on `Status` needs a branch for each, or a default; `Status.IsTerminal` covers `StatusDiscarded`. `RetryPolicy.MaxErrors` and `Until` still end a run `StatusFailed`. A batch now counts a discarded job as failed: it fires `Catch` and, without `AllowFailures`, cancels the other jobs (before, it counted as cancelled, which fired no `Catch`); set `Batch.DiscardedAs` to `StatusCancelled` for the old behaviour. On sqlstore, after migration 004 a `Statuses: [StatusCancelled]` filter no longer returns discarded runs.
- `sqlstore`: new migration `005_run_labels` (the label index) and its backfill, which `Migrate` runs; a team applying `MigrationScripts` with its own tool must call `Client.IndexLabels` once after applying it, or label searches miss older runs. Run `Migrate` before starting the new version.
- `admin`: `Operations` and `Actions` gain `schedule_pause`, `schedules.pause`, `schedules.resume` and `ui.read`; an `Authorizer` that allow-lists actions must allow `ui.read` for the web UI to load.
- `admin`: `Operations` and `Actions` gain `discarded` and `discarded.list`; an `Authorizer` that switches on actions sees a new one, and should refuse unknown actions by default.
- `sqlstore`: new migration `006_schedule_history` (`<prefix>schedule_last`, backfilled for existing schedules, `<prefix>attempt_errors` and `<prefix>run_forced`) and the list `ubgo/workflow/sqlstore/output` with `001_output`. Run `Migrate` before starting the new version.
- `sqlstore`: new migrations `007_leader_archive`, `008_run_info_nodes` and `009_schedule_tenant`, and the lists `ubgo/workflow/sqlstore/journal` and `ubgo/workflow/sqlstore/bucket` with `001`. `Start` requires the tables of the new migrations and lists (leaders, the archive tables, run info, nodes, step cancels, schedule specs, unique holders, schedule tenants, the journal and the buckets), so run `Migrate` (or apply `MigrationScripts`) before starting the new version.
- `admin`: `Operations` and `Actions` gain `schedule_trigger`, `force`, `schedules.show`, `schedules.trigger`, `runs.force_complete`, `runs.force_fail` and `runs.output`; an `Authorizer` that allow-lists actions must allow them to use the new screens.
- `hatchet`: an app that stored the engine UUID of a named trigger gets a new ID the next time it calls `Schedule`, because the unmarked trigger is replaced once; re-read IDs from `Schedule`'s return value or from `Schedules`.
- Every driver's public `Enqueue` (and `EnqueueTx`, `EnqueueMany` and group dispatch) refuses a reserved label on a job or a chained job with `workflow.ErrInvalid` (`workflow.CheckJobLabels`), unless the package that owns the key allowed it on the context (`workflow.AllowReservedLabels`, `AllowReservedPrefix`); before, only the owning middleware refused them, and an app could set `ubgo.workflow/` labels on a plain `Enqueue`.
- `json.Marshal` of a `workflow.Schedule` now writes the versioned JSON form (the zone by its IANA name); before, it wrote the zone as `{}`, and such a document cannot be read back. Marshalling refuses `time.Local` and fixed zones.
- `redis`: the key layout changed (ready sets per queue and workflow, pending sets, new hash fields); drain a prefix before upgrading a running deployment. A schedule now fires only on a client that registered its workflow, not on any client of the prefix, and `Discard` ends a run `StatusDiscarded` instead of failed.

### Deprecated

- `workflowtest.NewRecorder(nil)` (standalone recording mode) is kept for compatibility; use `workflowtest.Fake` instead.

### Fixed

- `sqlstore`: a data race between `SetWorkers` and weighted queue claiming (both read and wrote a copy of `QueueConfig`).
- `memory`: `RunAll` called right after `Signal` finishes the signalled run; the wait is marked satisfied under the lock when the event is handed over, before the waiter goroutine wakes.
- `hatchet`: between a failed attempt and its retry the engine shows a run FAILED for a moment, and the driver read that as final, so `Status`, `Runs`, `Retry`, the run-finished hook and `BatchSource` could report a failure, and fire a batch's `Catch`, for a run that then succeeded. One function now judges every FAILED read: it is final when the driver marked the failure non-retryable, when the step used its registered retries, or when it failed more than the new `hatchet.Config.FailedSettle` ago (`DefaultFailedSettle`, 5s); otherwise the run reads queued, or running, as between attempts.
- `sqlstore`: the lease heartbeat starts before a claimed step's first database write, so a busy database no longer lets a live worker's lease expire before its handler starts.
- `sqlstore`: the SQLite driver dependency `modernc.org/sqlite` moves from v1.39.0 to v1.45.0 (with `modernc.org/libc` v1.67.6, the version it pins). v1.39.0 leaked the rows of a query whose context was cancelled, which kept a read transaction open, so a later migration lock on the same database could wait until its 15-minute timeout; v1.40.1 fixed that and v1.42.2 fixed a cancelled query interrupting a later one on the same connection. v1.45.0 is the newest release that still declares go 1.24.0, so the module's Go minimum is unchanged. `TestCancelledTxReleasesTheWriteLock` now pins the fix.
- `workflowtest`: the suite's wrapper for a driver without `CapRunStates` reported a retrying run from a driver that does not declare the capability, a timing flake in group `retry`.
- `hatchet`, `sqlstore`, `memory`: once `Done` closes (after `Drain`, a recycle, stop-when-idle or the end of the Start ctx), every call returns `ErrNotStarted`; on Hatchet and sqlstore the client was marked stopped a moment after `Done` closed, so calls could still reach the backend (seen as an `Enqueue` accepted after stop-when-idle on MySQL), and memory was in time only by accident. Conformance group `done` holds every driver to it.
- `failover`: a secondary's copy of a workflow kept `CronWindows` and `ScheduleWhen` without `Cron`, which validation refuses, so such a workflow could not be registered.
- `failover`: a workflow with `Cron` and a `Misfire` policy failed `Register` on the secondary; its copy now drops `Misfire` and `CronOverlap` with the other schedule fields.
- `failover`: a fired run's `LabelSchedule` is now the schedule ID the failover client hands out (prefixed, as in `Schedules` and `Tick.ScheduleID`) in `Runs`, `StepInfo` and run-finished events, so it can be passed back to `PauseSchedule`, `TriggerSchedule` or a run query; a `LabelSchedule` filter in `Runs` and `CancelAll` is translated to each backend's own ID.
- `sqlstore`: found by the benchmark, a permanent deadlock when more workers than pool connections finished steps at once (Postgres' default pool is 8): every successful step read the run's chain on the pool while its record transaction held a connection. The chain is now read only when a step edits it, on the step's goroutine before any transaction opens. It was in the code since chain edits (`bd39f7b`).
- `hatchet`: a named `Schedule`'s `ScheduleID` is stable across a changed definition: it has the form `named:...` and names the (workflow, name) pair, not the engine trigger, as on the other drivers; `Unschedule`, `PauseSchedule`, `ResumeSchedule`, `TriggerSchedule` and `Schedules` resolve it by name, engine-UUID IDs still work, and a replaced trigger keeps its pause when `Config.API` is set. Earlier releases returned a new ID for a changed definition, which the conformance upsert case refuses; that case runs only with `WFTEST_REAL_CRON`, which earlier gates did not set.
- `hatchet`: rate limits are per workflow and per limit. The engine keys a dynamic bucket by the key's value alone across the tenant, so two workflows (or two limits) whose keys gave the same value shared one bucket, and one workflow's runs could wait for another's; the driver now prefixes the value with the limit's declaration (namespace, workflow, index), proven by `TestRateLimitScopeLive`. The bucket names change, so windows in flight start fresh at deploy.
- `hatchet`: a step held in the engine's queue (by a rate limit, a concurrency limit or busy workers) for more than five minutes, the engine's default schedule timeout, was failed by the engine with no attempt and no error, against the contract that a held run is delayed, never dropped. Every step and the batch coordinator now declare `Config.ScheduleTimeout` (new, default `DefaultScheduleTimeout`, 24 hours), shown by `TestScheduleTimeoutLive`; the on-failure task keeps the engine's five minutes, because the SDK takes no options for it.
- `sqlstore`: a claimed step could sit leased to nobody for a full lease (30 seconds by default) when reading it after the claim failed, for example because the database refused a new connection, and the takeover then counted as a reclaim toward `WithMaxReclaims`. The claim and the read are now one transaction, so a failed read leaves the step claimable at once.
