# Capabilities

A driver declares what it can really do as a [`workflow.Caps`](ds:block?id=caps-ssth52ga) set, returned by [`Client.Capabilities()`](ds:block?id=client-capabilities-wnp994at). `Start` compares it with what each registered workflow needs ([`CheckCaps`](ds:block?id=checkcaps-eg44ezuh)) and refuses a workflow the driver cannot honour with an [`UnsupportedError`](ds:block?id=unsupportederror-46jkqrxt) naming the missing capabilities, and `Enqueue` and `Schedule` do the same for a job or schedule ([`CheckJob`](ds:block?id=checkjob-2rsfu9ma), [`CheckScheduleCaps`](ds:block?id=checkschedulecaps-45emsz38)). A missing feature is an error at start-up, never a field silently dropped at run time. A capability is claimed only when the conformance suite proves it against the real backend.

## Capability matrix

Y means the driver declares the capability: each driver column is that driver's own list (memory [`caps`](ds:block?id=memory-caps-yxkuq583), sqlstore [`caps`](ds:block?id=sql-caps-yu37ef2j), hatchet [`caps`](ds:block?id=hatchet-caps-d6x9fv8z) and [`Client.capabilities`](ds:block?id=hatchet-client-capabilities-3ms5edtr), redis [`caps`](ds:block?id=redis-caps-bfgdst9t)). The Fake column is [`workflowtest.NewFake()`](ds:block?id=workflowtest-newfake-5kxdhuwb) with no options ([`fakeDefaultCaps`](ds:block?id=workflowtest-fakedefaultcaps-cgj8yc2b)); [`FakeCaps`](ds:block?id=workflowtest-fakecaps-grfyqkrc) and [`FakeLike`](ds:block?id=workflowtest-fakelike-my3v73nf) change it. A failover client declares the intersection of its two backends, with the exceptions in the last column ([`failover.Client.Capabilities`](ds:block?id=failover-client-capabilities-cb8n9pqm)).

Notes on the columns:

- **memory** keeps everything in the process, so its [`caps`](ds:block?id=memory-caps-yxkuq583) never holds `CapDurable`; it refuses expression keys (`workflow.KeyExpr`) at `Start`, which no capability names.
- **hatchet** implements `OnRunFinished` by polling the engine and its [`caps`](ds:block?id=hatchet-caps-d6x9fv8z) does not hold `CapRunFinished`: an event can be missed. Hatchet evaluates crons in UTC only and never runs a missed tick, so a zone or a misfire policy is refused. Its other refusals are listed in the `hatchet` package documentation.
- **hatchet** declares `CapPauseWorkflow` and `CapSchedulePause` only when `Config.API` ([`Client.capabilities`](ds:block?id=hatchet-client-capabilities-3ms5edtr)) (the engine's REST client, `hatchet.NewAPI`) is set; there `PauseAll()`, `PauseQueue` and `PauseSchedule` of `All` are refused.
- **Fake** claims `CapDiscardExhausted` only when [`FakeCaps`](ds:block?id=workflowtest-fakecaps-grfyqkrc) or `FakeLike` adds it (it validates the field and runs no retries), and never claims `CapQueueLimitGlobal` or `CapDrainReport`, whatever they say ([`fakeUnserved`](ds:block?id=workflowtest-fakeunserved-jwdtfx8n)).
- **failover** and the `middleware.WrapClient` and `WithCodec` wrappers do not forward `DrainWithReport`: `workflow.DrainTimeoutReport` on them still drains, and returns `detailed` false, meaning the report is unknown (not that nothing was cut off). Drain the backend clients directly when you need the report.
- **redis** keeps runs, schedules, signals, queues and leases in Redis and its [`caps`](ds:block?id=redis-caps-bfgdst9t) holds every capability but `CapClosure` (a Go function cannot be stored), `CapQueueLimitGlobal` (a run is claimed whole, so a queue's in-flight attempts are not counted across clients) and `CapSlotCost` (for the same reason a step cannot hold several worker slots; a workflow with `Step.Slots` above one is refused at `Start`). Its `CapDurable` is as strong as the Redis deployment's persistence settings ([The Redis driver](../drivers/redis.md)).
- **Fake** claims by default the memory driver's set ([`fakeDefaultCaps`](ds:block?id=workflowtest-fakedefaultcaps-cgj8yc2b)) without `CapDiscardExhausted`, `CapSlotCost`, `CapPayloadSearch` and `CapRoundRobin` (which `FakeCaps` can add) and without the capabilities it cannot serve by itself ([`fakeUnserved`](ds:block?id=workflowtest-fakeunserved-jwdtfx8n)): `CapRunFinished` (it runs nothing, so no run ever finishes), `CapRunQuery`, `CapLabelPrefix`, `CapCancelAll`, `CapSchedulePause`, `CapScheduleList`, `CapTickEvents`, `CapDrain`, `CapWorkerScale`, the bookkeeping it cannot do (`CapScheduleHistory`, `CapErrorHistory`, `CapEnqueueOnly`, `CapShutdownRequeue`), what only running steps gives (`CapSnooze`, `CapNodeIdentity`, `CapDueQuery`, `CapResult`, `CapTickTime`), and `CapQueueLimitGlobal` and `CapDrainReport`. [`FakeCaps`](ds:block?id=workflowtest-fakecaps-grfyqkrc) and [`FakeLike`](ds:block?id=workflowtest-fakelike-my3v73nf) drop the same set. It records `Retry`, `Delete` and `Pause` itself.
- **failover** treats the schedule family ([`scheduleCaps`](ds:block?id=failover-schedulecaps-kg7jvm5x): `CapCron`, `CapScheduleWhen`, `CapScheduleWindow`, `CapSchedulePause`, `CapScheduleList`, `CapTickEvents`, `CapOverlapSkip`, `CapScheduleTrigger`, `CapScheduleHistory`, `CapCronSeconds`, `CapScheduleActive`, `CapScheduleKeep`, `CapTickTime`) as the primary's alone unless `WithSchedulesOnBoth`, and `CapClosure` and `CapWorkerScale` ([`primaryOnlyCaps`](ds:block?id=failover-primaryonlycaps-wycqgwrv)) as the primary's alone always.
- **regional** ([`regional.Client.Capabilities`](ds:block?id=regional-client-capabilities-858xwx63)) declares the intersection of every region's capabilities, never `CapClosure` or `CapWorkerScale` ([`excludedCaps`](ds:block?id=regional-excludedcaps-vuc7vjgy)), and `CapRunFinished` only when every region implements `workflow.RunNotifier`.
- `RunEditor` (edit a scheduled run), `RunRescheduler`, `RunResetter` (`ResetRun`), `RunResetterWith` (`ResetRunWith`), `RunForker` (`ForkRun`) and `TransientSignaller` (`SignalWaiting`) are optional interfaces without a capability bit: a client implements them or not, and each answers for itself ([`workflow.Forward`](ds:block?id=forward-e32a7p36) forwards them; [`workflow.Supports`](ds:block?id=supports-4xyjjwvz) with `OpResetRun`, `OpResetSucceeded`, `OpForkRun`, `OpSignalWaiting` asks first). `RunSignalReader` (`PendingSignals`, `TakeSignal`, for `OnSignals`; memory, sqlstore and Redis; `Supports(c, OpRunSignals)`) and `RunSignaller` (`SignalRun` for wrappers whose refs carry a prefix: `regional`, `failover`, `cutover`) have no bit either.
- **hatchet** has no `RunSignalReader`, so `OnSignals` is refused at `Use`; it refuses `Concurrency.Weight` and its [`caps`](ds:block?id=hatchet-caps-d6x9fv8z) does not hold `CapRoundRobin` (its group round-robin is not proven live here).

## Reading and checking a set

| API | What it does |
|---|---|
| [`caps.Has(want)`](ds:block?id=caps-has-z5rv5wur) | True when `caps` holds every capability in `want` |
| [`caps.Missing(required)`](ds:block?id=caps-missing-yt996zkr) | The capabilities in `required` that `caps` lacks; `IsZero()` when none |
| [`caps.String()`](ds:block?id=caps-string-rrz2ge6p) | The names joined by `\|`, such as `Durable\|Cron`; the empty set is `none` |
| [`workflow.CapsOf(a, b, ...)`](ds:block?id=capsof-mmp8bkmj), [`caps.With(...)`](ds:block?id=caps-with-jwfj42qg), [`caps.Without(...)`](ds:block?id=caps-without-bkp9zq5y) | Build a set, add to one, take from one (a `Caps` is a value: each returns a new set) |
| [`caps.HasAny(o)`](ds:block?id=caps-hasany-n3gjxxvw), [`caps.Intersect(o)`](ds:block?id=caps-intersect-5vawyy93), [`caps.IsZero()`](ds:block?id=caps-iszero-pmummwj2) | Overlap, intersection, emptiness; two sets compare with `==` |
| [`workflow.AllCaps()`](ds:block?id=allcaps-umeq9egq) | Every capability the release declares |
| [`workflow.ParseCaps(s)`](ds:block?id=parsecaps-vryjfj24) | Reads the `String()` form back; JSON uses the same form and still reads the v0.1 number ([`UnmarshalJSON`](ds:block?id=caps-unmarshaljson-dngyx9tw)) |
| [`workflow.Requires(wf)`](ds:block?id=requires-6tcjbcfj) | What a driver needs to run `wf` as written (the "Needed by" column) |
| [`workflow.RequiresJob(job)`](ds:block?id=requiresjob-mrfs7vqd) | What a job needs: `CapDelayedStart` for `RunAt`, `CapIdempotencyKey` for a key, even under `IdempotencyOff`, so a key is never dropped without an error; `CapQueue` for a `Queue`, `CapJobRetry` (and the retry capabilities of its policy) for a `Retry`, `CapTenant` for a `Tenant`, and `CapChain` plus what each chained job needs for a `Chain` |
| [`workflow.CheckJob(have, job)`](ds:block?id=checkjob-2rsfu9ma), [`CheckJobQueue(have, wf, job)`](ds:block?id=checkjobqueue-nkkxjr77) | The `UnsupportedError` a driver returns from `Enqueue`: the job's own needs, then `CapQueueOverride` when the job leaves its workflow's queue |
| [`workflow.RequiresPause(t)`](ds:block?id=requirespause-bpxg35v9), [`CheckPause(have, t)`](ds:block?id=checkpause-9fyvd7wv) | What a `PauseTarget` needs (`CapPauseWorkflow` for a workflow or all, `CapPauseQueue` for a queue) and the refusal |
| [`workflow.RequiresSchedule(s)`](ds:block?id=requiresschedule-wk3hrhfy) | What a schedule needs: `CapCron`, `CapCronZone` outside UTC, its misfire policy's capability, and `CapScheduleWindow`, `CapOverlapSkip`, `CapCronSeconds`, `CapScheduleActive`, `CapScheduleKeep` or `CapTenant` when it sets `Windows`, `OverlapSkip`, `Seconds`, `Active`, `KeepRuns` or `Tenant` |
| [`workflow.CheckCaps(have, wf)`](ds:block?id=checkcaps-eg44ezuh), [`CheckScheduleCaps(have, s)`](ds:block?id=checkschedulecaps-45emsz38) | The `UnsupportedError` a driver returns from `Start` and `Schedule`, or nil |

A Caps holds up to 256 capabilities ([`capBits`](ds:block?id=capbits-tqwzzyzd)); a new one takes the next free number, which is never reused. [`CapWorkflowVersion`](ds:block?id=capworkflowversion-6gd7k448) is 61, [`CapSlotCost`](ds:block?id=capslotcost-9fatdg72) 62, [`CapPayloadSearch`](ds:block?id=cappayloadsearch-ebrgjdkj) 63, [`CapWaitOwnRun`](ds:block?id=capwaitownrun-2fd6b64v) 64, [`CapRoundRobin`](ds:block?id=caproundrobin-yw4jqugm) 65 and [`CapKeyWeights`](ds:block?id=capkeyweights-vqknb9qc) 66.

The names in [`String()`](ds:block?id=caps-string-rrz2ge6p) and in an `UnsupportedError` are the constant names without `Cap`: `Durable`, `DelayedStart`, `WaitForEvent`, `DurableSleep`, `ConcurrencyKey`, `RateLimit`, `IdempotencyKey`, `Cron`, `Cancel`, `RunFinished`, `RetryJitter`, `RetryDelay`, `MisfireSkip`, `MisfireFireOnce`, `MisfireFireAll`, `CronZone`, `Queue`, `QueueOverride`, `QueueLimitGlobal`, `JobRetry`, `RetryUntil`, `RetryMaxErrors`, `Discard`, `DiscardExhausted`, `RunQuery`, `RetryRun`, `DeleteRun`, `CancelAll`, `PauseWorkflow`, `PauseQueue`, `SchedulePause`, `ScheduleWhen`, `ScheduleWindow`, `TickEvents`, `ScheduleList`, `Drain`, `DrainReport`, `RunStates`, `Chain`, `WorkerScale`, `Closure`, `LabelPrefix`, `OverlapSkip`, `ScheduleTrigger`, `ScheduleHistory`, `EnqueueOnly`, `ErrorHistory`, `ShutdownRequeue`, `ForceRun`, `Snooze`, `Heartbeat`, `Tenant`, `RunTimeout`, `NodeIdentity`, `KeyFIFO`, `DueQuery`, `Result`, `CronSeconds`, `ScheduleActive`, `ScheduleKeep`, `TickTime`, `WorkflowVersion`, `SlotCost`, `PayloadSearch`, `WaitOwnRun`, `RoundRobin`, `KeyWeights`.
